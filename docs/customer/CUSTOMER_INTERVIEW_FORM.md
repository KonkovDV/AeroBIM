## Current process

1. How do you reconcile IFC / drawings / TZ / calculations today?  
2. Who owns the final «fit for purpose» decision?  
3. What tools are already in the loop (CDE, clash, IDS, OCR, …)?  

## Pain & priority

4. Top 3 recurring issue classes on completed packages?  
5. Which severity labels do you use today? (map later to Critical / Major / Minor / Info)  
6. What would make a 40-minute local walkthrough worth scheduling?  

## Data & security

7. Can a completed section stay on-prem for the walkthrough?  
8. Cloud LLM for advisory remarks — allowed / forbidden / case-by-case?  
9. Any PII / personal data constraints on stamps or sheets?  

## Success criteria (customer words)

10. What would «useful» look like after one section?  
11. Who must approve publishing any comparison numbers? (**Customer approval required**)
