## Who we are

AeroBIM team, Techlab Moscow program (Moscow Innovation Cluster), Samolet task track. Open core: github.com/KonkovDV/AeroBIM.

We align available package sources and show discrepancies with source links. **Documentation fitness decision stays with your specialist.**

## Limited pilot (what we propose)

**About 40 minutes on your side.** System runs locally (Docker). You load **one completed section** (IFC + text-layer PDF; native DWG is not promised).

- If you **have** a remarks reference — we walk matches / extras / misses. Walkthrough, not a product-accuracy claim.
- If you **do not** — we show one finding with provenance (sheet, GUID, rule). You keep the verdict.

Files need not leave your perimeter. The report stays with you regardless of outcome. `summary.passed=false` on the training slice is fail-closed Shared-gate, not “the product is broken.”

For a design organization, the same walkthrough can use an archival package under **your** NDA.

This is a **limited pilot / discovery measurement** — not a production rollout, not an SLA commitment, not a published accuracy claim.

## What we measure on the walkthrough

If a remarks reference exists: matches, extras, misses, criticality gap. Not dual-rater. Not product accuracy.

If none: one finding with provenance. Expert decides.

## What we do not claim from fixtures

Product accuracy, «>90%», native DWG readiness, delivered MEP clash, independent engineering calculation, CDE-ready BCF, or customer SLA copied from synthetic benchmarks.
